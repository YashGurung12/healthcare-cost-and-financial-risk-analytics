
The primary objective of this project is to analyze patient encounter, procedure, payer, and organizational data to understand the financial and operational factors influencing healthcare costs. By integrating data from multiple healthcare tables, the project aims to identify patterns of high-cost healthcare utilization, evaluate insurance reimbursement performance, and assess financial risks associated with patient encounters.

The analysis focuses on transforming raw healthcare data into meaningful business insights that can support hospitals and healthcare administrators in making informed decisions regarding patient care, resource allocation, and financial planning.

---
## Specific Objectives

### 1. Evaluate Patient Encounter Costs
Analyze the distribution of healthcare costs across different encounter classes (such as emergency, inpatient, ambulatory, wellness, and urgent care) to identify which encounter types contribute most to overall healthcare expenditure.

### 2. Identify High-Cost Patients
Detect patients who experience repeated high-cost encounters within a given time period. Understanding these utilization patterns helps healthcare providers develop targeted care management programs for patients requiring frequent or complex medical services.

### 3. Assess Financial Risk from Insurance Coverage
Measure the gap between the total claim cost and the amount reimbursed by insurance providers (payers) to identify encounters, diagnoses, and payers associated with significant uncovered costs. This analysis highlights areas where hospitals may experience financial risk due to insufficient reimbursement.

### 4. Analyze Diagnosis-Driven Healthcare Costs
Examine the relationship between diagnosis codes (ReasonCodes) and healthcare costs to determine which medical conditions are associated with higher treatment expenses and financial risk.

### 5. Understand Demographic Risk Factors
Analyze patient demographics—including age, gender, race, ethnicity, and marital status—to identify population groups that are more likely to experience high-cost encounters. These insights can support preventive healthcare initiatives and targeted patient management strategies.

### 6. Evaluate Procedure Cost Patterns
Assess the costs of medical procedures performed during patient encounters and determine how different procedures contribute to total healthcare spending. This helps identify resource-intensive treatments and opportunities for operational optimization.

### 7. Measure Payer Performance
Compare insurance providers based on their reimbursement patterns, coverage percentages, and uncovered costs to evaluate the effectiveness of payer contributions across different procedures and diagnoses.

### 8. Analyze Healthcare Organization Performance
Compare hospitals and healthcare organizations based on encounter volume, average encounter cost, encounter duration, and financial risk to identify operational differences and benchmarking opportunities across organizations.

### 9. Monitor Encounter Duration
Calculate the duration of patient encounters across different encounter classes and healthcare organizations to identify prolonged hospital stays that may indicate operational inefficiencies or increased resource utilization.

### 10. Develop Interactive Business Dashboards
Present analytical findings through interactive Tableau dashboards that enable stakeholders to monitor key performance indicators, explore financial trends, identify high-risk patient groups, and support evidence-based decision-making.

---

# Business Value
This project delivers value to healthcare organizations by:
- Identifying major drivers of healthcare costs and financial risk.
- Improving visibility into insurance reimbursement performance.
- Supporting efficient allocation of clinical and financial resources.
- Enabling proactive management of high-cost patient populations.
- Providing insights into demographic and diagnosis-based healthcare utilization.
- Helping administrators monitor operational performance across healthcare organizations.
- Supporting strategic planning through data-driven healthcare analytics.
